# Level 3 Real-World Practice: Shift Lead Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student has finished every task in Track 4 —
Growing at Work (Lessons 1–3) — in the simulator. Not after each
individual lesson. This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who
skips it still moves straight on to Level 4. Offer it during office hours,
or post it to Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

**Why bundled, not per-lesson:** same reason as every level so far — one
focused real-world assignment beats three small ones trickling in, one
context-switch per skill.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post **one** assignment — "Level 3
      Wrap-Up" — with all of the following attached together:
  - a short written meeting-invite scenario (Lesson 1)
  - a shared Google Drive folder for the sharing scenario (Lesson 2)
  - a real Google Sheet with a wrong-looking total (Lesson 3)
- [ ] One combined instruction sheet, not three — use the student
      instructions below as-is or adapt them.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 3 in the simulator — nice work as a shift lead. If
you'd like extra practice, try the same three things for real, in one
sitting:
1. Write the reply you'd actually send to the meeting-invite scenario —
   accept, decline, or propose a new time — and say why.
2. Share the matching file from the shared Drive folder with your
   teacher's email, at the right access level.
3. Check the real Google Sheet's total the same way you practiced — if
   it's wrong, write one sentence saying what looks off.

Turn it all in together.

**Optional extra challenge:** on #1, suggest two alternative times instead
of one, the way a busy person actually negotiates a reschedule.

## Teacher review
Same approach as every level so far — skim for completion, not
perfection, and give one or two specific notes back rather than a grade.

## Confidence check-in — end of Level 3
The app already asked a version of this at the end of Lesson 3 — no need
to repeat it, but it's worth reading the student's answers with them, if
you get the chance during office hours:
- "Out of everything since the promotion (calendar, files, spreadsheets),
  the thing I feel most ready for is: ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend starting this whole program is: ___________"
