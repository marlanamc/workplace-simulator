# Level 23 Real-World Practice: Put It All Together Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 23).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 24, the final level of the currently-written
curriculum. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google Classroom
for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): a real (or realistic mock) version of
      the same packet — a number to check, a calendar note, a short
      write-up, sent together.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 23 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, put together the real version of the same packet, the way
you practiced.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — a coherent packet that actually ties the pieces
together (not three disconnected attachments) is the goal.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 24 — the closing reflection — as soon as
  `ops-report-packet` shows complete in their Objectives panel, whether or
  not they did this optional practice.
