# Lesson 1: Where You've Been

**Time:** ~15-20 minutes · **Level:** 24 — Where You've Been · **Track:**
23 — Where You've Been
**Task key:** `portfolio-reflection` · **Status:** not built yet. This is
the closing level of the currently-written curriculum (Acts I-VII) — no
new software skill, a reflection and a shareable summary instead.
**Prerequisite:** Level 23 complete

## We will learn...
- look back across everything practiced — every app, every promotion, from
  answering one email as a brand-new hire to running a full weekly report
  as a Team Lead — and put together a short, real summary a student could
  actually show a real employer.

## Before class (teacher prep) — once built
- [ ] Simulator: a review screen listing every certificate/track the
      student has earned across the whole program, plus a short guided
      write-up (3-4 prompts) that feeds into their shareable certificate
      page (the existing `/certificate/[learnerId]` route).
- [ ] No Google Classroom post needed yet — this is optional, supplementary
      practice bundled into the Level 24 wrap-up (`real-practice-
      assignment.md`). Never a gate — a student is done with the
      currently-written curriculum either way. This level works well done
      together during office hours, if that suits the student better than
      alone.

## Simulator practice (~15-20 min) — once built
1. Look through the full list of certificates/tracks earned — every app,
   every promotion, start to finish.
2. Answer 3-4 short reflection prompts (see below).
3. Review the shareable certificate page it generates — this is something
   the student can genuinely show a real employer or a college advisor.

## Confidence check-in — reflection for the whole program
- "Out of everything in this whole program, the thing I feel most ready
  for at a real job is: ___________"
- "The thing I'm still building confidence in is: ___________"
- "If I could tell myself something on Day One (Level 1), it would be:
  ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend thinking about starting this program:
  ___________"

## Notes for the teacher
- This is the real capstone of the currently-written curriculum — treat it
  as a genuine milestone, not just another wrap-up. Take the time to
  actually look at a student's certificate page with them if you can; for
  many students, this may be the first document they have that says,
  concretely, what they can do.
- The program isn't necessarily "over" here — more acts can always be
  written and added later (see `00-scope-and-sequence.md`'s roadmap for
  what's beyond Level 24). But everything written and scoped as of this
  pass ends here, and it's a complete, meaningful arc on its own.
