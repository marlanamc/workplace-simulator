# Capture a changed assignment

**Task key:** `meeting-minutes` · **Status:** implemented; learner pilot pending.

Use the selected course route and existing level. All references needed for this activity are supplied in the simulator; unrelated acts are not prerequisites. Instructions, Help, and recovery belong in the bilingual Job Card.

## Simulator practice

1. Prepare a short agenda, then read the simulated huddle. The full transcript is available beside both notes and follow-up; clicking through each line is optional.
2. Record decisions and actions.
3. Notice the correction: Riley is away Thursday; Alex owns training on Friday.
4. Prepare the follow-up and select the final owner and day for every action.

## What completion demonstrates

Transferring final owners and days into the structured action list, including a correction. Agenda and notes checks establish basic completeness, not fidelity or writing quality; those remain available for optional feedback.

Learners may move between agenda, notes, and follow-up in any order. Labeled return controls retain in-session drafts. This is not a promise that unfinished drafts survive a page reload.

Wrong choices should lead to useful Job Card recovery. There is no timer or mandatory teacher approval. Optional real-world practice is separate from simulator completion. Use a fresh example in the learner pilot to assess transfer.
