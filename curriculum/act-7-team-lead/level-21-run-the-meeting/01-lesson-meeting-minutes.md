# Lesson 1: Run the Meeting, Start to Finish

**Time:** ~15-20 minutes · **Level:** 21 — Run the Meeting · **Track:** 20
— Run the Meeting
**Task key:** `meeting-minutes` · **Status:** not built yet. This is the
promotion into Team Lead.
**Prerequisite:** Act VI complete (the environment refreshes here: a new
title, and for the first time, the story lets the student lead rather than
just participate)

## We will learn...
- run a meeting the whole way through — agenda before, brief notes during,
  and a follow-up email with clear action items after — the full loop,
  not just one piece of it like Level 8's `team-meeting` was.

## Before class (teacher prep) — once built
- [ ] Simulator: reuse Level 8's agenda-writing step, then a short mock
      "meeting" (a scripted back-and-forth or a few key points appearing
      one at a time) where the student jots 2-3 quick notes, then a
      follow-up email screen listing who owes what.
- [ ] No Google Classroom post needed yet — this is optional, supplementary
      practice bundled into the Level 21 wrap-up assignment
      (`real-practice-assignment.md`), offered once this lesson is done.
      Never a gate — a student who skips it still moves on.

## Simulator practice (~15-20 min) — once built
1. Write a short agenda, the same skill from Level 8.
2. During the mock meeting, jot 2-3 quick notes on what was decided — not
   a full transcript, just the key points.
3. Notice who agreed to do what.
4. Send a follow-up email listing the action items, who owns each one, and
   by when — the same "clear, short, actionable" writing habit from every
   report/status email so far.

## Notes for next time
- The follow-up email is the actual point of this lesson — a meeting
  without a clear "who does what by when" afterward is easy to let drift.
  Don't let the notes-taking step eat the whole session; keep it brief.
