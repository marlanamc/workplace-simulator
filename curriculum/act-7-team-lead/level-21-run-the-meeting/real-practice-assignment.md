# Level 21 Real-World Practice: Run the Meeting Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 21).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 22. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): pair students up (or use a small group)
      for a real short meeting the student leads, with a real agenda and a
      real follow-up email afterward.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 21 in the simulator — congratulations on becoming
a Team Lead. If you'd like extra practice, lead a real short meeting
(even a 5-minute one) and send a real follow-up email afterward.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — a follow-up email with clear owners and a rough
timeline is enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 22 as soon as `meeting-minutes` shows
  complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this
  optional practice.
