# Level 22 Real-World Practice: The Review Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 22).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 23. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice. This is a good
one to keep purely as a conversation rather than written homework, if that
suits the student better.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a short mock profile, similar to
      the simulator one but different details, for extra practice.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 22 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, write one more strength/area-to-grow note for a different
mock profile, or just talk it through with your teacher during office
hours instead.

## Teacher review
Skim for tone, not correctness — look for specific and constructive, not
generic or harsh.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 23 as soon as `performance-review` shows
  complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this
  optional practice.
