# Level 2 Real-World Practice: Settling In Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student has finished every track in Level 2 in
the simulator — Schedules & Documents and Judgment & Follow-Through
(Lessons 1–5). Not after each individual lesson. This is **optional and
supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves straight on to Level
3. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google Classroom for anyone
who wants the extra practice; it should never hold anyone back.

**Why bundled, not per-lesson:** doing a full round-trip to a real document
after every single simulator task was exhausting — by the time a student
built real momentum in the app, going back to real practice constantly
broke the flow. Now, for anyone who wants it, there's one focused
real-world assignment covering the whole level at once.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post **one** assignment — "Level 2
      Wrap-Up" — with all of the following attached together (a folder, or
      one combined doc/slide deck, works best):
  - a view-only mock schedule with one conflict (Lesson 1)
  - a mock time card with one wrong-looking total (Lesson 2)
  - a mock pay stub PDF (Lesson 3)
  - a short incident scenario (Lesson 4)
  - a policy excerpt/scenario for the handbook skill (Lesson 5)
- [ ] One combined instruction sheet, not five — use the student
      instructions below as-is or adapt them.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 2 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like extra
practice, try the same five things for real, in one sitting if you can:
1. Look at the mock schedule, find the conflict, and write the message
   you'd send to ask for a swap.
2. Check the mock time card — does the total look right? Write what you'd
   say if not.
3. Find the net pay on the mock pay stub.
4. Write up the incident scenario, in order, as if for your manager.
5. Find the answer to the handbook scenario and say where you found it.

Turn it all in together — one submission, not five.

**Optional extra challenge** (for students who finish quickly or have prior
office experience): on #1, suggest a specific person or day to swap with,
not just "I can't work this"; on #3, calculate what the hourly rate must
have been.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection — the simulator already did the graded
practice. This is about confirming the skill holds up outside the guided
environment. One or two specific notes back (not a grade) is plenty:
"this part landed well," "try this next time."

## Confidence check-in — end of Level 2
The app already asked a version of this at the end of Lesson 5 — no need to
repeat it, but it's worth reading the student's answers with them, if you
get the chance during office hours:
- "Out of everything we've practiced (schedules, pay stubs, incident
  reports, the handbook), the thing I feel most ready for is: ___________"
- "The thing I still want more practice with is: ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend starting this class: ___________"

## Notes for the teacher
- Some students will finish all five in one sitting; others may need it
  split across two days, or skip it entirely — any of those is fine.
- A student is ready for Level 3 (Lesson 6 on, "Shift Lead") as soon as
  every Level 2 task shows complete in their Objectives panel, whether or
  not they did this optional practice.
