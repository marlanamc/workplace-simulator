# Level 1 Real-World Practice: New Hire Wrap-Up

**When to assign:** only after a student has finished every track in Level 1
in the simulator — Getting Started, Schedules & Documents, and Judgment &
Follow-Through (Lessons 1–6). Not after each individual lesson. This
replaces the old lesson-by-lesson "Part B" homework.

**Why bundled, not per-lesson:** doing a full round-trip to real email or a
real document after every single simulator task was exhausting — by the
time a student built real momentum in the app, going back to real practice
constantly broke the flow. Now they finish the whole level, get one focused
real-world assignment, and start Level 2 with a clean slate.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Google Classroom: post **one** assignment — "Level 1 Wrap-Up" — with
      all of the following attached together (a folder, or one combined
      doc/slide deck, works best):
  - a short practice email written *as if* from a supervisor (Lesson 1)
  - a view-only mock schedule with one conflict (Lesson 2)
  - a mock time card with one wrong-looking total (Lesson 3)
  - a mock pay stub PDF (Lesson 4)
  - a short incident scenario (Lesson 5)
  - a policy excerpt/scenario for the handbook skill (Lesson 6)
- [ ] One combined instruction sheet, not six — use the student instructions
      below as-is or adapt them.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt)
You just finished Level 1 in the simulator — nice work. Now do the same six
things for real, in one sitting if you can:
1. Reply to the practice email using your real email, the same way you
   practiced.
2. Look at the mock schedule, find the conflict, and write the message
   you'd send to ask for a swap.
3. Check the mock time card — does the total look right? Write what you'd
   say if not.
4. Find the net pay on the mock pay stub.
5. Write up the incident scenario, in order, as if for your manager.
6. Find the answer to the handbook scenario and say where you found it.

Turn it all in together — one submission, not six.

**Optional extra challenge** (for students who finish quickly or have prior
office experience): on #1, also cc a second person and confirm a specific
time; on #2, suggest a specific person or day to swap with, not just "I
can't work this"; on #4, calculate what the hourly rate must have been.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection — the simulator already did the
graded practice. This is about confirming the skill holds up outside the
guided environment. One or two specific notes back (not a grade) is plenty:
"this part landed well," "try this next time."

## Confidence check-in — end of Level 1
The app already asked a version of this at the end of Lesson 6 — no need to
repeat it, but it's worth reading the student's answers with them:
- "Out of everything we've practiced (email, schedules, pay stubs, incident
  reports, the handbook), the thing I feel most ready for is: ___________"
- "The thing I still want more practice with is: ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend starting this class: ___________"

## Notes for the teacher
- Some students will finish all six in one sitting; others may need it
  split across two days. Either is fine — the point is one wrap-up moment,
  not one moment per skill.
- Once this is turned in, the student is ready for Level 2 (Lesson 7 on) —
  a new environment, a new "week" at Harborside Cafe, and their first real
  shift-lead responsibilities.
