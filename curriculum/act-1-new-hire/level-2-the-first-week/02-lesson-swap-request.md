# Lesson 2: Ask for a Shift Swap Through a Form

**Time:** ~5 minutes · **Level:** 2 — The First Week · **Track:** 2 — The First Week
**Task key:** `swap-request` · **Status:** built — the Portal's Shift Swap
tab has its own request form: a dropdown for which shift, a second dropdown
for the shift you could work instead, and an optional reason field. Both
dropdowns are graded — see below.
**Prerequisite:** Lesson 1 (`schedule`)

## We will learn...
- act on the conflict found in Lesson 1 by filling out a real workplace form,
  and propose a fix that actually solves the problem for both sides.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Simulator: nothing to set up — the Shift Swap tab shows the same week
      the student just read, with the Thursday clash highlighted.
- [ ] Know the answer before class: the doctor is Thursday at **11:00 AM**,
      and the shift is **10:00 AM – 6:00 PM**. The only option that works is
      the **late Thursday shift, 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM**. Every other choice is
      refused with a reason.

## Simulator practice (~5 min)
1. Open the **Browser** → **Employee Portal** → **Shift Swap**.
2. Look at the schedule shown above the form.
3. In the first dropdown, choose the shift that clashes — **Thursday**.
   Choosing any other shift is refused: that one was never the problem.
4. In the second dropdown, choose a shift you could work instead. Only the
   **late Thursday shift** clears the 11 AM appointment. The early Thursday
   shift still covers it, and moving to another day either lands on a day
   off or doubles up a day already worked.
5. Optionally, write a short reason.
6. Click **Submit request**.

## Confidence check-in

- "I could suggest a fix that works for me *and* for my manager." 🙂 😐 🙁

## Notes for next time
- The heart of this lesson is step 4. Asking to be taken *off* a shift is
  easy; proposing the specific shift you can work instead is what makes a
  request easy to approve, and it's the difference between "I can't work
  Thursday" and "could I do Thursday late instead?"
- Students often reach for a different day first. That instinct is worth
  discussing: the cafe still needs Thursday covered, so a same-day swap is
  the smallest possible ask.
