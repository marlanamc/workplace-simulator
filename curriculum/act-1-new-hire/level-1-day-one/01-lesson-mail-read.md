# Lesson 1: Read Your Supervisor's Email

**Time:** ~4 minutes · **Level:** 1 — Day One · **Track:** 1 — Getting Started
**Task key:** `mail-read` · **Status:** built and playable today
**Prerequisite:** Level 0 (`tour`) so they have seen Help, Next, and the
shift list. Track 0, including 0.5 login safety, if they are new to a
work login (or teacher confirms they can open a browser tab, type, and
will not share a password)

## We will learn...
*(read aloud / post on board — plain language, one idea)*
- find the right email, read it, and know what the person is asking for. No
  writing yet — that's the next lesson.

## Simulator practice (~4 min)
1. Sign in with your name, PIN, and class code, then open **Mail**.
2. Find the email from Maria Delgado in the inbox — there are other emails
   in there too, on purpose. A wrong pick gets a friendly reminder pointing
   at hers.
3. Open it and read it.
4. Answer the question the app asks: what does Maria need? Pick the answer
   that matches what you just read.
5. That's it — no reply yet. This lesson is only "find it and understand it."

*Nothing here is real. Every wrong click just gets a friendly reminder, never
a penalty.*

## Notes for next time
- If students want to "just reply already," that's a good sign — tell them
  Lesson 2 is exactly that, right after this one, same inbox.
- Splitting reading from writing means a wrong answer here tells you the
  student didn't understand the ask, not that they can't type — a different
  problem than Lesson 2's.
