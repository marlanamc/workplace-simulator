## We will learn...

- write a short reply to a manager's email, in your own words.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Simulator: nothing to set up — this picks up in the same Mail inbox
      as Lesson 1.

## Simulator practice (~4 min)
1. Open **Mail**. Maria's email is already there — this time, click **Reply**
   (not Forward — a coaching toast will point that out if you pick the
   wrong one, that's OK).
2. Write a short reply that answers what she asked for. Sentence starters
   are there if you want them.
3. Click **Send**. No file needed yet — that's next lesson.
4. If unsure at any point, click the **Help** button — it won't count
   against you.

*Nothing here is real. There is no way to make a mistake that matters.*

## Confidence check-in
