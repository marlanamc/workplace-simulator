# Level 3 Real-World Practice: Payday & Trouble Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student has finished every task in Level 3 in
the simulator — timeclock, pay stub, and the normal-shift review
(Lessons 1–3). This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who
skips it still moves straight on to Act II. Offer it during office hours,
or post it to Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

**Why bundled, not per-lesson:** same reasoning as every other level here —
one focused assignment covering the whole level, offered once, rather than
a round-trip after each short lesson.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post **one** assignment — "Level 3
      Wrap-Up" — with a mock time card (one wrong-looking total) and a mock
      pay stub PDF attached together.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 3 in the simulator — that's all of Act I done.
If you'd like extra practice, try these for real:
1. Check the mock time card — does the total look right? Write what you'd
   say if not.
2. Find the net pay on the mock pay stub.

Turn it in as one submission.

**Optional extra challenge:** calculate what the hourly rate must have
been, given the hours and net pay on the mock stub.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection — the simulator already did the graded
practice. One or two specific notes back is plenty.

## Notes for the teacher
- This is also a good moment for the Act I real-world "bridge out": if a
  student hasn't already sent a real email with a real attachment from
  Level 1's done screen, this is a natural nudge to do it before Act II
  starts.
- A student is ready for Act II as soon as every Level 3 task shows
  complete in their My Job panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
