# Lesson 3: A Normal Shift

**Time:** ~4 minutes · **Level:** 3 — Payday & Trouble · **Track:** 3 — Payday & Trouble
**Task key:** `shift-review` · **Status:** built — three quick recap
questions covering schedule, timeclock, and pay stub, back to back, with no
new tool introduced.
**Prerequisite:** Lesson 2 (`paystub`)

## We will learn...
- nothing new. This lesson checks that the 8 skills from Day One and The
  First Week actually stuck, by mixing them together in one short shift.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Simulator: nothing to set up — this is a short quiz-style recap, not
      a new task type.
- [ ] Tell students up front that this lesson has no new tool — some
      students relax visibly once they hear that, which is the point.

## Simulator practice (~4 min)
1. Open the next job on the desktop: "A normal shift."
2. Answer three short questions, one at a time: where to find your
   schedule, what to do at the end of a shift, and how to check a pay
   stub. Each is multiple choice — a wrong pick just gets a quick reminder.
3. Finishing all three completes Act I and triggers the Shift Lead
   promotion.

## Confidence check-in
- "I can do all of this without anyone showing me." 🙂 😐 🙁
- "Which of the 8 Act I skills do I feel least sure about?"
