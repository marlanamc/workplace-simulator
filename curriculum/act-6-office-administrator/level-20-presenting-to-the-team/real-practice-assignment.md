# Level 20 Real-World Practice: Presenting to the Team Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 20, and
all of Act VI). This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who
skips it still moves straight on to Level 21 and Act VII. Offer it during
office hours, or post it to Google Classroom for anyone who wants the
extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): have the student build a real 3-slide
      deck (Google Slides) using a number from any real spreadsheet they
      have access to, or a mock one you provide.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Act VI — congratulations on completing the Office
Administrator arc. If you'd like extra practice, build a real 3-slide deck
and present it to a partner or the class.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — a clear title, an accurate number, and a one-
sentence takeaway is enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- This is a genuine milestone — five full acts complete (New Hire, Shift
  Lead, Shift Supervisor, Assistant Manager, Office Administrator). See
  `00-scope-and-sequence.md` for what's next: Act VII (Team Lead capstone,
  Levels 21-24) is the final stretch of the currently-written curriculum.
- A student is ready for Level 21 as soon as `slide-deck` shows complete
  in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
