# Lesson 1: Build and Present a Short Deck

**Time:** ~15-20 minutes · **Level:** 20 — Presenting to the Team ·
**Track:** 19 — Presenting to the Team
**Task key:** `slide-deck` · **Status:** not built yet. This is the Act VI
capstone — finishing it completes the Office Administrator arc.
**Prerequisite:** Level 19 complete

## We will learn...
- build a very short slide deck (3 slides: title, one data slide, one
  summary slide) and present it — pulling a real number from work already
  done (a budget or report total from earlier levels) instead of starting
  from nothing.

## Before class (teacher prep) — once built
- [ ] Simulator: a simple slide editor (title text, one chart/number slide
      pre-populated with a total from an earlier level's sheet, one
      "takeaway" slide with a short text box) plus a mock team-meeting
      screen to "present" in.
- [ ] No Google Classroom post needed yet — this is optional, supplementary
      practice bundled into the Level 20 wrap-up assignment
      (`real-practice-assignment.md`), offered once this lesson is done.
      Never a gate — a student who skips it still moves on.

## Simulator practice (~15-20 min) — once built
1. Open the slide editor and fill in a short, clear title.
2. On the data slide, check the number that's already pulled in — does it
   match what you'd expect? (Same "check it, don't just trust it" habit
   from every spreadsheet lesson so far.)
3. Write one short takeaway sentence on the summary slide — what should
   the team actually do with this information?
4. "Present" it in the mock meeting screen — read through your three
   slides out loud, in order.

## Notes for next time
- Three slides, not more — the skill is "make a clear, short point with
  real data," not deck-design. Keep this tight so it doesn't turn into a
  PowerPoint-mechanics lesson.
