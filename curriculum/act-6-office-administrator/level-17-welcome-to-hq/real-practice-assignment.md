# Level 17 Real-World Practice: Welcome to HQ Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 17).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 18. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a real, larger shared Drive folder
      with a couple of similarly-named files to find using search.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 17 in the simulator — congratulations on the
promotion to Office Administrator. If you'd like extra practice, find the
right file in the real shared folder using search, the way you practiced.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — check the student found the right *version*, not
just a file with a similar name.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 18 as soon as `office-drive` shows complete
  in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
