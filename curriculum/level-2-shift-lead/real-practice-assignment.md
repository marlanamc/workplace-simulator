# Level 2 Real-World Practice: Shift Lead Wrap-Up

**When to assign:** only after a student has finished every task in
Track 4 — Growing at Work (Lessons 1–3) — in the simulator. Not after each
individual lesson.

**Why bundled, not per-lesson:** same reason as Level 1 — one focused
real-world assignment at the end of the level beats three small ones
trickling in, one context-switch per skill.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Google Classroom: post **one** assignment — "Level 2 Wrap-Up" — with
      all of the following attached together:
  - a short written meeting-invite scenario (Lesson 1)
  - *(once built)* a shared Google Drive folder for the sharing scenario
    (Lesson 2)
  - *(once built)* a real Google Sheet with a wrong-looking total
    (Lesson 3)
- [ ] One combined instruction sheet, not three — use the student
      instructions below as-is or adapt them.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt)
You just finished Level 2 in the simulator — nice work as a shift lead. Now
do the same three things for real, in one sitting:
1. Write the reply you'd actually send to the meeting-invite scenario —
   accept, decline, or propose a new time — and say why.
2. *(once built)* Share the matching file from the shared Drive folder with
   your teacher's email, at the right access level.
3. *(once built)* Check the real Google Sheet's total the same way you
   practiced — if it's wrong, write one sentence saying what looks off.

Turn it all in together.

**Optional extra challenge:** on #1, suggest two alternative times instead
of one, the way a busy person actually negotiates a reschedule.

## Teacher review
Same approach as Level 1 — skim for completion, not perfection, and give
one or two specific notes back rather than a grade.

## Confidence check-in — end of Level 2
The app already asked a version of this at the end of Lesson 3 — no need
to repeat it, but it's worth reading the student's answers with them:
- "Out of everything since the promotion (calendar, files, spreadsheets),
  the thing I feel most ready for is: ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend starting this whole program is: ___________"
