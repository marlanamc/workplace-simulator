# Level 5 Real-World Practice: Covering More Ground Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 5). This
is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves
straight on to Level 6, the promotion into Shift Supervisor. Offer it
during office hours, or post it to Google Classroom for anyone who wants
the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post one short assignment with two
      unrelated mock requests (a scheduling conflict and a file-sharing
      ask) arriving "at the same time."

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 5 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like extra
practice, try handling two real (mock) requests in one sitting, the way you
practiced.

**Optional extra challenge:** add a third small request and see how it
feels — most students find this noticeably harder than two, which is worth
noticing and talking about.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — the point is that both requests got handled, not
which order.

## Notes for the teacher
- This closes out Act II ("Shift Lead") of the roadmap in
  `00-scope-and-sequence.md`. Level 6 is the promotion into "Shift
  Supervisor" — a new environment, new responsibilities (scheduling for
  others, not just yourself).
- A student is ready for Level 6 as soon as `triage` shows complete in
  their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional practice.
