# Level 19 Real-World Practice: The Expense Report Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 19).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 20, the Act VI capstone. Offer it during office
hours, or post it to Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra
practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a real Google Sheet expense report
      and a few mock receipts, one missing.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 19 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, match the real receipts to the real report and flag the
missing one, the way you practiced.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — correct matches and catching the missing receipt is
enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 20 as soon as `expense-report` shows
  complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this
  optional practice.
