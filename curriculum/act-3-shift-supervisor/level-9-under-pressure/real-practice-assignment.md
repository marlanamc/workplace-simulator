# Level 9 Real-World Practice: Under Pressure Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 9, and
all of Act III). This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who
skips it still moves straight on to Level 10 and the promotion into
Assistant Manager. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google
Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post three short, unrelated mock
      scenarios (a complaint, a coverage gap, a scheduling conflict)
      arriving "at the same time."

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Act III — congratulations on becoming a Shift
Supervisor. If you'd like extra practice, handle all three real (mock)
situations in one sitting, the way you practiced, and say in one sentence
what you handled first and why.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — the point is that all three got a reasonable
response, not one "correct" order.

## Notes for the teacher
- This is a genuine milestone — treat it as one, not just another wrap-up.
  A student is ready for Level 10 ("An Offer," the start of Act IV —
  Assistant Manager) as soon as `priority-call` shows complete in their
  Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional practice.
