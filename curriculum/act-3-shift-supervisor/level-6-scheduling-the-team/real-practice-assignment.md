# Level 6 Real-World Practice: Scheduling the Team Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 6). This
is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves
straight on to Level 7. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google
Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a mock weekly schedule grid (a real
      Google Sheet) with one coverage gap and a short crew-hours reference.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 6 in the simulator — congratulations on the
promotion to Shift Supervisor. If you'd like extra practice, fill the real
mock schedule's gap the same way you practiced, and write the heads-up
email you'd send.

**Optional extra challenge:** two people could reasonably cover the gap —
write one sentence explaining why you picked the one you did.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — look for a reasonable pick (someone with room in
their week), not one "correct" answer.

## Notes for the teacher
- Welcome to Act III (Shift Supervisor) of the roadmap in
  `00-scope-and-sequence.md`. Levels 6-9 build toward a second promotion,
  into Assistant Manager, at the end of Level 9.
- A student is ready for Level 7 as soon as `team-schedule` shows complete
  in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
