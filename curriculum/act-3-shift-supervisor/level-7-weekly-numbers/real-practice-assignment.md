# Level 7 Real-World Practice: Weekly Numbers Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 7). This
is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves
straight on to Level 8. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google
Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a real Google Sheet with a week of
      numbers and a `SUM` formula whose range is missing one row.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 7 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like extra
practice, find and fix the same kind of mistake in the real sheet, and
write the email you'd send flagging it.

**Optional extra challenge:** write the `AVERAGE` formula yourself from
scratch instead of just checking one that's already there.

## Teacher review
Check that the student actually opened the formula (not just retyped the
number) and can explain in one sentence what was wrong with the range.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 8 as soon as `formula-check` shows complete
  in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
