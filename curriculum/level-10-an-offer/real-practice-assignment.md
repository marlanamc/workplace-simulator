# Level 10 Real-World Practice: An Offer Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 10).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 11. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a short mock offer letter with a
      day/time that conflicts with a mock work schedule.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 10 in the simulator — congratulations on the
promotion to Assistant Manager. If you'd like extra practice, write a real
reply accepting the mock offer, and add the class to a real calendar next
to a mock work schedule, the way you practiced.

**Optional extra challenge:** if a student is actually considering a real
program like Bunker Hill's Transitions to College Program, this is a good
moment to point them toward it directly — the simulator content is meant
to build comfort with the *kind* of task, not replace looking into the
real thing.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection — a professional-toned reply and a
calendar entry that reflects a real class time is enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 11 as soon as `college-offer` shows
  complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this
  optional practice.
