# Level 8 Real-World Practice: First Team Meeting Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 8). This
is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves
straight on to Level 9. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google
Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a short prompt describing a
      real (mock) reason a small group needs to meet.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 8 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like extra
practice, create a real Google Calendar invite for a mock meeting with a
short agenda attached, the way you practiced.

**Optional extra challenge:** invite the meeting to a specific room or add
a video-call link, if your class has access to one.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — a clear time, the right people invited, and a
2-3 bullet agenda is enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 9 — the Shift Supervisor capstone — as soon
  as `team-meeting` shows complete in their Objectives panel, whether or
  not they did this optional practice.
