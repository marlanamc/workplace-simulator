# Lesson 6: Look Something Up Under Pressure

**Time:** ~5 minutes · **Level:** 1 — New Hire · **Track:** 3 — Judgment & Follow-Through
**Task key:** `handbook` · **Status:** built — the Handbook tab opens straight
into a planted scenario ("you're sick tomorrow — what's the call-out
policy?") with a multiple-choice check pinned below the same search/browse
view used in the earlier exploration pass.
**Prerequisite:** Lesson 5

## We will learn...
- find an answer in the employee handbook instead of guessing, when it
  actually matters.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Simulator: nothing to set up — the Handbook tab always opens the same
      scenario and question.
- [ ] Google Classroom: this is the last lesson in Level 1 — post the
      combined **Level 1 wrap-up assignment** now (see
      `06a-level1-real-practice.md`), covering this skill and every other
      Level 1 skill together, in one sitting.

## Simulator practice (~5 min)
1. Read the scenario prompt at the top of the **Handbook** tab.
2. Search or browse to the right article.
3. Answer the question pinned at the bottom, based on what the handbook
   actually says — a wrong pick gets a friendly reminder to check again, not
   the answer.
4. This is the last task in Track 3 — finishing it awards the "Judgment &
   Follow-Through" certificate **and** completes Level 1. The desktop and
   Objectives panel will reflect the move into Level 2 once a student is
   ready to continue.

## Confidence check-in — end-of-level reflection
By the end of this lesson a student has, on their own, used WorkMail, the
Employee Portal, PDF Reader, the Incident Report, and the Handbook — every
app in Level 1. There's no separate "capstone" exercise; finishing this
level already is one. This reflection now happens once, here, instead of
being repeated at the end of every track:

- "Out of everything we've practiced (email, schedules, pay stubs, incident
  reports, the handbook), the thing I feel most ready for is: ___________"
- "The thing I still want more practice with is: ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend starting this class: ___________"

## Notes for the teacher
- There's no single "pass" state for this level — the goal is watching a
  student move between tools independently, which is the actual skill a new
  lead needs (not any one app in isolation).
- Use the reflection answers, plus which tracks/certificates a student has
  earned so far (visible any time in their Objectives panel), to decide which
  lesson to loop back to with each student individually, rather than moving
  the whole class forward in lockstep.
- Assign the Level 1 wrap-up (`06a-level1-real-practice.md`) now, before
  moving anyone on to Level 2 (Lesson 7).
