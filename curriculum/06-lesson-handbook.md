# Lesson 6: Look Something Up Under Pressure

**Time:** 20 minutes total (10 + 10) · **Track:** 3 — Judgment & Follow-Through
**Task key:** `handbook` · **Status:** not built yet — the Handbook tab exists
and is browsable/searchable today, but there's no graded scenario driving a
student to it under a time-pressure prompt.
**Prerequisite:** Lesson 5

## We will learn...
- find an answer in the employee handbook instead of guessing, when it
  actually matters.

## Before class (teacher prep) — once built
- [ ] Simulator: a scenario that requires checking the handbook to answer
      correctly (e.g., "You're sick tomorrow — what's the call-out policy?"
      or "A coworker asks about the break rules — what do you tell them?"),
      with the Handbook tab one click away.
- [ ] Google Classroom: post the same style of scenario, with the real
      handbook excerpt (or the class's actual policy doc, if appropriate) to
      search.

## Part A: Simulator (~10 min) — once built
1. Read the scenario prompt.
2. Open **Handbook**, search or browse to the right article.
3. Answer based on what the handbook actually says — not a guess.

## Part B: Real Practice (~10 min)
1. Read the same style of scenario in Google Classroom.
2. Search the real policy document.
3. Write or say the answer, citing where you found it.

## Confidence check-in — end-of-track reflection
By the end of this lesson a student has, on their own, used WorkMail, the
Employee Portal, PDF Reader, the incident form, and the Handbook — every app
in the simulator. There's no separate "capstone" exercise; finishing this
track already is one.

- "Out of everything we've practiced (email, schedules, pay stubs, incident
  reports, the handbook), the thing I feel most ready for is: ___________"
- "The thing I still want more practice with is: ___________"
- "One thing I'd tell a friend starting this class: ___________"

## Notes for the teacher
- There's no single "pass" state for this track — the goal is watching a
  student move between tools independently, which is the actual skill a new
  lead needs (not any one app in isolation).
- Use the reflection answers, plus which tracks/certificates a student has
  earned so far (visible any time in their Objectives panel), to decide which
  lesson to loop back to with each student individually, rather than moving
  the whole class forward in lockstep.
