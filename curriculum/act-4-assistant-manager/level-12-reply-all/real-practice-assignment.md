# Level 12 Real-World Practice: Reply All Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 12, and
all of Act IV). This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who
skips it still moves on whenever Act V (elective) or Act VI content is
ready. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google Classroom for
anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a real multi-person email thread
      (a copied/mocked one) with a casual draft reply for the student to
      edit.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Act IV — congratulations on completing the Assistant
Manager arc. If you'd like extra practice, edit the real draft reply into
something professional and decide who it should actually go to.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — check the tone edit and the audience decision
(reply vs. reply-all), not word-for-word phrasing.

## Notes for the teacher
- This is a genuine milestone — four full acts complete (New Hire, Shift
  Lead, Shift Supervisor, Assistant Manager). See
  `00-scope-and-sequence.md` for what comes next: Act V is an optional
  elective (college-prep or healthcare-admin), and Act VI (Office
  Administrator) picks up the main ladder from here — both still at the
  planning stage, not yet written lesson-by-lesson.
- A student is considered done with the currently-written curriculum once
  `reply-all` shows complete, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
