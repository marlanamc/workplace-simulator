# Lesson 1: An Offer

**Time:** ~15-20 minutes · **Level:** 10 — An Offer · **Track:** 11 — An
Offer
**Task key:** `college-offer` · **Status:** implemented; learner pilot pending. This is the
promotion into Assistant Manager, and the story beat where Harborside
offers to pay for a Bunker Hill Community College Business Essentials
class.
**Prerequisite:** Level 9 complete (the environment refreshes here: new
title, new emails, an Assistant Manager's first week)

## We will learn...
- read a formal offer carefully, reply to accept it professionally, and
  add a personal commitment (a class) to a calendar that's already full of
  work shifts — the first time work and outside-of-work responsibilities
  have to share the same calendar.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Simulator: an email in Mail from Maria/HR laying out the offer —

      That day/time overlaps one of the student's regular shifts.

## Simulator practice (~15-20 min)
1. Open **Mail** and read HR's offer: Harborside pays for one BUS 101
   class in Spring 2027. The rules: register by Friday, December 11, 2026;
   the class cannot be during your shifts unless your manager approves a
   change; keep a C or better; reply with your section.
2. Open the **BHCC schedule**. Three sections: 01 (Mon/Wed mornings), 02
   (Tue 5:00-7:45 PM, open seats), 03 (Thu evening, online, full with a
   waitlist). Compare them with your spring shifts in **Calendar** and
   choose one. Nothing on screen says which one works.
3. Section 02 is during your Tuesday close. Write to Renata: say when the
   class meets and ask for a shift change, before registration closes.
4. Add the class to Calendar: Tuesday 5:00-7:45 PM, weekly, from the first
   day of classes (Tuesday, January 19, 2027).
5. Reply to HR: accept, and name Section 02 or its CRN.

## Notes for next time
- This lesson is explicitly framed to the student as a short taste of the
  kind of digital task college expects — reading an offer, replying
  professionally, managing a shared calendar — not a simulation of an
  actual semester. Real programs like Bunker Hill's Transitions to
  College Program exist for that; this app is a 15-20-minute-a-week
  on-ramp, not a replacement for one.
