# Level 11 Real-World Practice: The Budget Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 1 (all of Level 11).
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 12. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a real Google Sheet budget with an
      `IF` status column and a chart, one category over budget.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 11 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, find the over-budget category in the real sheet and write
the flag-it email the way you practiced.

**Optional extra challenge:** try changing one of the actual numbers and
watch the status and chart update — a good way to see the formula and
chart are actually connected, not just decoration.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — correctly identifying the over-budget category and
the amount is enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 12 as soon as `budget-sheet` shows complete
  in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
