# Lesson 4: Read a Pay Stub

**Time:** 20 minutes total (10 + 10) · **Track:** 2 — Schedules & Documents
**Task key:** `paystub` · **Status:** built — opening the most recent pay
stub from the Employee Portal opens the real PDF in PDF Reader, then two
quick multiple-choice checks (net pay, then total hours) confirm the student
found the right numbers.
**Prerequisite:** Lesson 3 (comfortable checking that hours match)

## We will learn...
- find your net pay on a pay stub and confirm the hours match what you worked.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Simulator: nothing to set up — the most recent pay stub always opens
      the same document and the same two check questions.
- [ ] Google Classroom: post a real-looking mock pay stub PDF with 2–3
      findable facts (gross pay, a deduction, net pay).
- [ ] Prepare 1–2 questions, e.g.: *"What is the net pay?"* / *"How many
      overtime hours were paid?"*

## Part A: Simulator (~10 min)
1. Open the **Browser** → **Employee Portal** → **Pay Stubs**.
2. Click the most recent one — it opens as a real document in **PDF Reader**.
3. Read the net pay and the hours listed, then switch back to the **Browser**.
4. Answer both quick questions (net pay, then total hours) — a wrong pick
   just gets a friendly reminder to look again.

## Part B: Real Practice (~10 min)
1. Open the mock pay stub PDF from Google Classroom.
2. Find the net pay and answer the prepared question(s).
3. Tell your teacher, or write the answer on a handout.

**Optional extra challenge:** calculate what the hourly rate must have been,
given the hours and gross pay shown.

## Confidence check-in
- "I could find my net pay on a real pay stub." 🙂 😐 🙁
- "If the numbers didn't match what I expected, I would: ___________"

## Notes for next time
- This is a good diagnostic for reading-comprehension and basic-math pacing —
  some students will need the document simplified or read aloud; that's a
  content fix (a simpler mock pay stub), not a skill fix.
