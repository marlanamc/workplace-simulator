# Level 15 Real-World Practice: Staying On Top of It Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes whichever path's lesson they
chose (Path A `coursework` or Path B `billing-sheet`). This is **optional
and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves straight on to
Level 16. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google Classroom for
anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional), Path A: post a short mock syllabus
      section with a due date and a submission box.
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional), Path B: post a real Google Sheet with a
      billing mismatch to catch.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 15 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, try the real-world version of whichever path you chose:
- **Path A:** find the due date and submit a short response in a real
  online classroom tool, the way you practiced.
- **Path B:** find the mismatched row in the real sheet and write the
  flag message you'd send.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 16 as soon as either path's task shows
  complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this
  optional practice.
