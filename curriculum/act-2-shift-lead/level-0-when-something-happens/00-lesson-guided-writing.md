# Lesson: Say What Happened (guided writing warm-up)

**Time:** ~5–8 minutes · **Act II, Level 0** — comes BEFORE the incident report
**Task key:** `guided-writing` (not in code yet)
**Status:** not built yet
**Prerequisite:** Level 3 complete (has typed short replies and form answers)

## Why this lesson exists
The incident report is the course's first open free-writing under a
professional-tone requirement — everything before it was clicks, multiple
choice, or one-sentence replies with sentence starters. That is a cliff.
This lesson is the step in between: the same "what happened" writing, but
every sentence starts from a chip.

## We will learn...
*(read aloud / post on board — plain language, one idea)*
- tell what happened in three short sentences, in order: first, then, now

## Simulator practice (~5–8 min) — once built
1. A short scene plays out in text: a customer spilled a drink near the
   register. Nobody is hurt.
2. Three boxes, in order, each with sentence-starter chips ("First…",
   "Then…", "Now…"). Tap a chip, finish the sentence — or type your own.
3. The app checks only two things: each box has a sentence, and the order
   buttons match what happened. Tone is NOT graded here — that pressure
   waits for the incident lesson.
4. Done card: "You told what happened, in order. That is the whole skill.
   Next time it will be a real report form."

## Nudges (write these into the build)
- Empty box on submit → "One short sentence is enough. Start with a chip
  if you want."
- Events out of order → "Read it back. Did the spill happen first, or the
  mop?"

## Confidence check-in
- "I can say what happened in order, in writing." 🙂 😐 🙁

## Notes for the teacher
If offering this in class instead of the app: same three boxes on paper,
same chips on the board. The chips are the lesson — order and completeness,
not grammar.
