# Levels 4–6 Real-World Practice: Lead Tools Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Level 6 (The Numbers) in the
simulator — Calendar, Drive, and Sheets, one sitting each. Not after each
level. This is **optional and supplementary**. A student who skips it still
moves on. After these three tools they may keep going in Act II, **stay
and lead** (Acts III–IV), start **healthcare / front desk** (Act V Path B),
or start **office / admin** (Act VI).

**Why bundled:** one real round-trip covering the three lead tools beats
three small Classroom posts.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post **one** assignment — "Lead tools
      wrap-up" — with:
  - a short written meeting-invite scenario (Level 4)
  - a shared Google Drive folder for the sharing scenario (Level 5)
  - a real Google Sheet with a wrong-looking total (Level 6)
- [ ] One combined instruction sheet.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just practiced the tools a lead uses — in a cafe, a store, cleaning,
or a job site. If you'd like extra practice, try the same three things
for real, in one sitting:
1. Write the reply you'd send to the meeting-invite scenario — accept,
   decline, or propose a new time — and say why.
2. Share the matching file from the shared Drive folder with your
   teacher's email, at **Can view**, not **Can edit**.
3. Check the real Google Sheet's total. If it's wrong, write one sentence
   saying what looks off.

Turn it all in together.

**Optional extra challenge:** on #1, suggest two other times, the way a
busy person actually reschedules.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection. One or two specific notes back.

## Notes for the teacher
- These three tools are the shared trunk. Name that out loud for students
  who are not staying in food service.
- Reporting In (Level 7) and Covering More Ground (Level 8) are extra
  Act II practice. They are not required before healthcare or office.
