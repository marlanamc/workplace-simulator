# Level 4 Real-World Practice: Reporting In Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes Lesson 2 (all of Level 4). This
is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves
straight on to Level 5. Offer it during office hours, or post it to Google
Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post one short assignment — a real
      Google Sheet (or Doc) shared as **Viewer**, plus an email address to
      report a total to (and a second address to cc).

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 4 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like extra
practice, try the same thing for real:
1. Open the shared file. Confirm it is view only / read only — you should
   not be able to type in it.
2. **File → Make a copy.** Give your copy a clear name.
3. Write the total formula yourself in *your* copy (if it's a Sheet).
4. Send an email reporting the total, with a second person cc'd.

**Optional extra challenge:** write one sentence in the email explaining
*why* the total looks the way it does (e.g., "tips were higher Thursday
because of the concert downtown") — not just the number itself.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection. Check they worked in a *copy*, not
the shared original, and that the formula actually references the right
range, not just that a number appears.

## Notes for the teacher
- This level is two short lessons on purpose: first the lock-and-copy
  habit, then the formula + cc. Together they should still feel like one
  focused 15-20 minute session.
- A student is ready for Level 5 as soon as `status-report` shows complete
  in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this optional
  practice.
