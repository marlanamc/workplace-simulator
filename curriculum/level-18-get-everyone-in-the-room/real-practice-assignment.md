# Level 18 Real-World Practice: Get Everyone in the Room Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes both Lesson 1
(`multi-person-scheduling`) and Lesson 2 (`video-call`) — all of Level 18.
This is **optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still
moves straight on to Level 19. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional): post a real multi-person Google
      Calendar scheduling scenario (a few mock calendars with busy blocks).

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 18 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, find a real time that works for 3-4 mock calendars, the
way you practiced. Lesson 2 (video-call etiquette) doesn't need a separate
real-world version — it's simulator-only by design.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion — a correctly-identified open slot is enough.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 19 as soon as both `multi-person-scheduling`
  and `video-call` show complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not
  they did this optional practice.
