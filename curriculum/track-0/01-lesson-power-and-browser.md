# Lesson 0.1: Turn It On, Open the Browser

**Time:** ~10 minutes · **Track 0** (before Level 1) · **Not a simulator level**
**Task key:** none — real Chromebook only
**Status:** ready to teach (no app build needed)
**Prerequisite:** none. This is the true starting line.

## We will learn...
- turn the computer on and off, on purpose
- open Chrome (the browser) and recognize it by its icon

Some students have never turned on a computer. Nothing about that is
obvious: the power button is hidden, the screen goes dark on its own, and
"the internet" and "the computer" feel like the same thing.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] One Chromebook per student (or pair), charged.
- [ ] Know where this model's power button is before class.
- [ ] Decide the class Wi-Fi story: already connected, or you connect them.

## Practice (~10 min) — real Chromebook
1. Find the power button. Press it. Watch what happens — nothing you press
   here can break the machine.
2. When the screen goes dark on its own, tap a key. Name it out loud:
   "asleep, not broken."
3. Find the Chrome circle (red, yellow, green, blue). Click it. That is
   the browser — the door to everything in this course.
4. Close the lid. Open it. The work is still there.
5. Turn it all the way off, then on again, so shutdown loses its mystery.

## Confidence check-in
- "I can turn this computer on by myself." 🙂 😐 🙁

## Notes for the teacher
One idea per step, spoken before it is read. The win condition is small on
purpose: power on, find Chrome. Everything else waits for 0.2.
