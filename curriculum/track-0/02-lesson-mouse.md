# Lesson 0.2: Click, Double-Click, Scroll

**Time:** ~10–15 minutes · **Track 0** (before Level 1) · **Not a simulator level**
**Task key:** none — real Chromebook only
**Status:** ready to teach (no app build needed)
**Prerequisite:** 0.1 (can turn it on and find Chrome)

## We will learn...
- point, click, and double-click with the touchpad (or a mouse)
- scroll up and down a page that is longer than the screen

Clicking is a physical skill, like pouring without spilling. It needs
repetitions, not explanation.

## Before class (teacher prep)
- [ ] Chromebooks from 0.1. External mice help students with hand pain —
      have two or three if you can.
- [ ] Pick one long, harmless page to scroll (a recipe site or the
      library's homepage works fine).

## Practice (~10–15 min) — real Chromebook
1. Rest one finger on the touchpad. Move the arrow to each corner of the
   screen. Slow is correct.
2. Click something safe ten times: a bookmark, the clock, an empty spot.
   Feel the press.
3. Two fingers, drag down: the page moves. Scroll to the bottom of the
   long page, then back to the top.
4. Double-click practice only if this device needs it — Chromebooks mostly
   don't. Do not teach a skill they won't use this month.
5. End with one real move: open Chrome, click one bookmark, scroll it.

## Confidence check-in
- "I can click the thing I am aiming at." 🙂 😐 🙁

## Notes for the teacher
Watch for a death-grip on the touchpad — it means the pace is too fast.
Repetition with easy targets first; small targets only after the big ones
feel boring.
