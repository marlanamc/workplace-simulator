# Level 14 Real-World Practice: The Paperwork Wrap-Up

**When to offer:** after a student finishes whichever path's lesson they
chose (Path A `financial-aid` or Path B `patient-intake`). This is
**optional and supplementary** — a student who skips it still moves
straight on to Level 15. Offer it during office hours, or post it to
Google Classroom for anyone who wants the extra practice.

## Before class (teacher prep, only if offering this)
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional), Path A: post a real or realistic mock
      financial aid award letter (PDF) with a findable amount and deadline.
- [ ] Google Classroom (optional), Path B: post a mock intake form and a
      "someone's asking to see this" scenario.

## Student instructions (post as-is, or adapt — optional)
You just finished Level 14 in the simulator — nice work. If you'd like
extra practice, try the real-world version of whichever path you chose:
- **Path A:** find the award amount and accept-by deadline on a real
  (or realistic mock) award letter.
- **Path B:** decide whether to share the mock intake form with the
  person asking, and write one sentence explaining your decision.

## Teacher review
Skim for completion, not perfection. On Path B, look for a clear reason
behind the decision, not just a yes/no.

## Notes for the teacher
- A student is ready for Level 15 as soon as either path's task shows
  complete in their Objectives panel, whether or not they did this
  optional practice.
