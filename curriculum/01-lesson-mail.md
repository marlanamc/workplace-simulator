# Lesson 1: Answer Your Supervisor

**Time:** 20 minutes total (10 + 10) · **Track:** 1 — Getting Started
**Task key:** `mail` · **Status:** built and playable today
**Prerequisite:** Track 0 (or teacher confirms student can open a browser tab and type)

## We will learn...
*(read aloud / post on board — plain language, one idea)*
- read a message from a manager, reply the right way, and attach the file she asked for.

## Part A: Simulator (~10 min)
1. Sign in with your name, PIN, and class code, then open the **Browser** →
   **WorkMail** tab.
2. Find the email from Maria Delgado in the inbox.
3. Open it and read it.
4. Click **Reply** (not Forward — a coaching toast will point that out if you
   pick the wrong one, that's OK).
5. Write a short reply that answers what was asked, then click **Attach file**
   and choose the safety report (not one of the similar-looking decoys).
6. Click **Send**.
7. If unsure at any point, click the **Help** button — it won't count against
   you. The 🎯 **Objectives** tab on the right edge always shows what task
   you're on if you forget.
8. Watch the points total in the tray go up, and the certificate pop-up —
   that's Track 1 done. Do it again ("Do it again" on the done screen) if you
   finish early.

*Nothing here is real. There is no way to make a mistake that matters — every
wrong click just gets a friendly reminder.*

## Part B: Real Practice (~10 min)
1. Open **Google Classroom**.
2. Find **"Lesson 1 Practice Email"** and open/download it.
3. Read it the same way you just practiced.
4. Open **your real email**.
5. Reply to your teacher's email (or, if the practice file isn't a real email,
   send a new email *to your teacher* answering the same question, using
   Reply/Send the same way you just practiced).
6. Send it.

*This step uses your real email account, but the only person who will see it
is your teacher — it's still a safe space to practice.*

**Optional extra challenge** (for students who finish quickly or have prior
office experience): also cc a second person (a classmate or a second teacher
email if available), and add one sentence confirming a specific time.

## Confidence check-in
The app already asks this on the "done" screen ("How do you feel about doing
this at work tomorrow?") — no need to repeat it, but it's worth reading the
student's answer with them:
- "I could reply to an email like this at a real job." 🙂 😐 🙁
- "The part that felt hardest was: ___________"
- "If this happened at work and I wasn't sure what to do, I would: ___________"

## Notes for next time
- If several students struggled with Reply vs. Forward, spend 2 minutes at the
  start of the next lesson reviewing that distinction before moving on.
- If students with more computer background finished Part A almost instantly,
  give them the cc/extra-challenge version of Part B next time rather than more
  simulator time.
